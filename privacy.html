HueHue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the HueHue app collects, why, who sees it and what you can do about it. It goes together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Arturo Notarnicola, Italy. Contact for any privacy question or request: arturonotarnicola@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

DataDetails
AccountWhen you sign in with Google we receive your email address and a Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
ProfileYour username, your colour, an optional profile photo, the stickers, frames and titles you choose, and your settings (private account, colour passport, notifications).
Your shotsThe photos you post with the in-app camera, their caption, score and colour data, and the date and time.
City (optional)Only if you turn on the colour passport: your phone uses your approximate location to find the name of the city, and we save only the city and country with your shot. We never save your exact location.
Social activityLikes, comments, who you follow and who follows you, and the accounts you block.
Game progressPoints, streaks, leagues, drops, rewards, events joined and the cities you have seen.
ReportsWhen you report a photo, comment or profile, or someone reports yours: the reason, who reported it, and a copy of the reported text or photo.
NotificationsA push token that identifies your device for notifications, and which kinds of notifications you want.
Anonymous usage dataHow the app is used (sessions, screens and actions, operating system), linked to a random ID created on your phone, not to your account or name.

We don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data for advertising, and we don't access your contacts or your photo library (except the single photo you pick as profile picture).

3. Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To run the game (your account, shots, scores, leaderboards, social features, notifications you asked for): necessary to provide the service you signed up for (contract).
  • To keep HueHue safe (handling reports, hiding content, blocking and banning accounts, preventing cheating and abuse): our legitimate interest and yours in a safe community.
  • To improve the app with anonymous usage data: our legitimate interest. This data doesn't identify you.
  • Location for the city and push notifications: your consent, given through your phone's permissions and the in-app settings. You can withdraw it at any time.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other players see your public profile (username, colour, profile photo, stickers, frames, title, streak, scores and league), your shots with caption and city, and your likes and comments. With a private account, your journal and shots are visible only to people you follow.
  • Service providers: HueHue runs on Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) for sign-in, database, photo storage, server functions and notifications. Our database is located in the EU (Milan, Italy). Some processing by Google may happen outside the EU, protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. On iPhone, notifications are delivered through Apple's push service. The city name is found by your phone's own location service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Likes and comments are deleted automatically after about 2 days.
  • Your account, profile, shots and game progress stay until you delete your account. We may also close accounts inactive for more than 2 years, after warning you.
  • Reports and copies of reported content are kept as long as needed to handle the report and any appeal, and at most 12 months.
  • Anonymous usage data is kept for at most 24 months.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Journal → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your shots and photos, your likes, comments, follows and blocks, and your notification tokens. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to arturonotarnicola@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Italy it is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

8. Children

HueHue is not for children under 13. In countries where the law requires an older age to use a social app without a parent's consent (for example 14 in Italy), younger users need a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child is using HueHue without permission, write to us and we will delete the account.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers, and access rules make sure each user can change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you, and the authorities, if a breach puts it at risk.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. For important changes we tell you in the app before they apply. The date at the top shows the latest version.
