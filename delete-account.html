Delete your HueHue account

HueHue · developer: Arturo Notarnicola

In the app

  1. Open HueHue and go to your Journal.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can't open the app, write to arturonotarnicola@gmail.com from the email address of your HueHue account, with your username. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete some data and keep your account

  • A shot: open it from your Journal and tap the delete icon at the top. The photo, its likes and comments are deleted from our servers and from the app.
  • A comment: tap ⋮ next to your comment and choose Delete. Under your own shots you can also delete other people's comments.
  • Your profile photo: Journal → Edit profile → tap the photo → Remove photo.
  • Cities: turn off Settings → Color passport and no city is saved with your next shots.
  • Anything else: write to arturonotarnicola@gmail.com and tell us what to delete; we do it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account and profile (username, colour, profile photo), your shots and photos, your likes, comments, follows and blocks, your game progress and your notification tokens. Deletion is permanent.

What may be kept

Reports about content you posted, with a copy of that content, for at most 12 months to handle safety and appeals; anonymous usage data, which isn't linked to your account, for at most 24 months. See the Privacy Policy.
